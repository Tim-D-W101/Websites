How much will it cost?

A business website is R7 500 once-off. Or pay less upfront with a monthly plan: R4 999 + R399/month with Care, or R3 999 + R699/month with Care Plus. Business tools start from R5 000. Tell us what you need and we’ll send a fixed quote. The price only changes if you ask for something new, and we quote that first.

Do I have to take a monthly plan?

No. Pay once-off and the first year of hosting is included. After that it’s R249 a year. Changes are R199 each.

What’s the free mock-up?

A sample of your website, built from your public info, like your Google Maps listing or Facebook page. It’s free and there’s no obligation.

Do I own it?

Yes. Once it’s paid in full, the website or tool is yours, and your domain is registered in your name.
