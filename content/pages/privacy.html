Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t set cookies and doesn’t use analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking. It doesn’t load fonts, scripts or images from other companies: everything comes from this site.

The quote form

The form doesn’t store or send anything by itself. When you press the button, it opens WhatsApp (or your email app) on your own device with a message already filled in. Nothing reaches us unless you press send.

The message contains what you typed:

  • what you need, your business name, type of business and town;
  • your description of what you need;
  • the link, rough budget and timing, if you added them;
  • your name and how you’d like us to reply, with your email address or number if you added one.

WhatsApp also shows us your phone number, and email shows us your email address.

Your message goes only to our WhatsApp or email. We use it only to reply to you, to quote, and to build your free mock-up or plan from your business’s public information.

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages). Like every web host, Cloudflare’s servers automatically record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the website and protect it from attacks. We don’t use it to identify you.

Cloudflare may process this information outside South Africa. It is covered by Cloudflare’s privacy policy and its data protection commitments.

WhatsApp and email

When you message us, your message is also handled by WhatsApp (owned by Meta) or by the email providers involved, under their own privacy policies.

If you become a client

To build and run your website or tool, we keep your business details, your contact details, the content you send us (like your logo, photos, prices and hours) and records of your payments.

If we build you a business tool, the data in it (like your clients, bookings or orders) belongs to you. We only access it to build, support or fix the tool, and we never use it for anything else. In POPIA terms, you are the responsible party for that data and we process it on your behalf.

We keep client records for as long as you’re a client, and afterwards only for as long as the law requires (for example, five years for tax records).

How long we keep enquiries

If you ask for a quote but don’t become a client, we delete your messages and any mock-up or plan within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask us:

  • what personal information we hold about you;
  • to correct it;
  • to delete it;
  • to stop using it.

To ask, send us a message on WhatsApp or by email. We’ll confirm once it’s done. If the law says we must keep something (like an invoice), we’ll tell you what and why.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. See how to lodge a complaint on their website, or email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.
