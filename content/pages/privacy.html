Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t set cookies and doesn’t use analytics, advertising pixels or any other tracking. It doesn’t load fonts, scripts or images from other companies: everything comes from this site.

The free mock-up form

The form doesn’t store or send anything by itself. When you press the button, it opens WhatsApp (or your email app) on your own device with a message already filled in. Nothing reaches me unless you press send.

The message contains what you typed: your business name, type of business, town, the link you added, your name and how you’d like me to reply (plus your email address or number, if you added one). WhatsApp also shows me your phone number, and email shows me your email address.

I use these details only to reply to you about your mock-up or question, and to build the mock-up from your business’s public information.

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Cloudflare (Cloudflare Pages). Like every web host, Cloudflare’s servers automatically record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the website and protect it from attacks. I don’t use it to identify you.

Cloudflare may process this information outside South Africa. It is covered by Cloudflare’s privacy policy and its data protection commitments.

WhatsApp and email

When you message me, your message is also handled by WhatsApp (owned by Meta) or by the email providers involved, under their own privacy policies.

If you become a client

To build and run your website, I keep your business details, your contact details, the content you send me (like your logo, photos, prices and hours) and records of your payments.

Payments are processed by PayFast. I never see or store your full card or bank details.

I keep client records for as long as you’re a client, and afterwards only for as long as the law requires (for example, five years for tax records).

How long I keep enquiries

If you ask for a mock-up but don’t become a client, I delete your messages and the mock-up within 12 months, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask me:

  • what personal information I hold about you;
  • to correct it;
  • to delete it;
  • to stop using it.

To ask, send me a message on WhatsApp or by email. I’ll confirm once it’s done. If the law says I must keep something (like an invoice), I’ll tell you what and why.

Complaints

If you’re unhappy with how I’ve handled your information, please tell me first so I can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa. See how to lodge a complaint on their website, or email POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za.
